# Riverside Heights — Cancellation, Refund and Transfer Policy

## Cancellation by the buyer
A buyer who cancels the booking must give written notice to the customer care desk. The developer may forfeit 10% of the total consideration and refunds the balance within 45 days of the cancellation request.

## Taxes on cancellation
Stamp duty, registration fees and GST already paid to the government are not refunded by the developer. The buyer may apply to the relevant authorities for any refund the law allows.

## Cancellation for non-payment
If a buyer does not pay an instalment for three consecutive demand letters, the developer may cancel the allotment after giving 15 days' written notice. The refund then follows the same rule as cancellation by the buyer.

## Refund if possession is not given
If the developer fails to give possession by the agreed date, including the grace period, the buyer may withdraw from the project. The developer then returns the full amount paid with interest, as provided under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016.

## Transfer of booking before possession
A buyer may transfer the booking to another person before possession with the developer's written consent. A transfer fee of ₹ 50,000 applies, and the new buyer signs a fresh Agreement for Sale on the same terms.

## Name change or adding a co-buyer
Adding a spouse, parent or child as a co-buyer, or correcting a name, is processed within 15 working days of a written request with supporting documents. No transfer fee applies to these changes.
