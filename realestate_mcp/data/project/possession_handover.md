# Riverside Heights — Possession and Handover Policy

## Possession date
The developer will hand over possession on or before 31 March 2028. A grace period of 6 months applies only for reasons beyond the developer's control, such as natural calamities or orders of a court or government authority.

## Delay compensation
If possession is delayed beyond the grace period, the developer pays the buyer interest at 9% per annum on the amount paid, for every month of delay, until possession is offered.

## Occupancy certificate and possession notice
Possession is offered only after the occupancy certificate is received. The developer then sends a possession notice by email and post, listing the balance payment and the documents to bring.

## Taking possession
The buyer should take physical possession within two months of the possession notice. Maintenance charges start from the date stated in the possession notice, whether or not the buyer has moved in.

## Pre-handover inspection
Before taking possession, the buyer may inspect the apartment with a site engineer and submit a snag list. Snag items are fixed within 15 days of the inspection.

## Handover kit
At handover the buyer receives the keys, the possession letter, the warranty cards for fitted appliances and a copy of the building's fire safety plan.

## Defects after possession
If the buyer finds a structural defect or a defect in workmanship, quality or services within 5 years of possession, the buyer should report it to the customer care desk. The developer will repair it free of charge within 30 days of the report.
