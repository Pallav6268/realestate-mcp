# Riverside Heights — Maintenance and Society Guide

## Maintenance charges
Maintenance charges are ₹ 3.50 per sq ft of carpet area per month, payable from the date stated in the possession notice. The first 12 months are payable in advance at the time of possession.

## Corpus fund
A one-time corpus fund of ₹ 50 per sq ft of carpet area is collected at possession and handed over to the association of allottees when it takes charge of maintenance.

## Association of allottees
The developer helps the buyers form an association of allottees once a majority of the apartments are booked. The developer manages maintenance until the association takes over the common areas.

## Amenities
Residents have access to a swimming pool, a gym, a children's play area, a community hall and a landscaped garden. The pool and gym are open from 6 am to 10 pm.

## Interior work and renovation
Interior work is allowed between 9 am and 6 pm from Monday to Saturday. Structural changes, such as removing walls or columns, are not allowed. Buyers must inform the facility office before starting any work.

## Pets
Pets are allowed. Owners must keep dogs on a leash in common areas and clean up after them.

## Move-in
Move-in with packers and movers can be scheduled with the facility office between 8 am and 8 pm. The service lift is reserved for moving goods.
