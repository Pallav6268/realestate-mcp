# Riverside Heights — Help and Escalation

## Customer care desk
For any question or request, write to helpdesk@example.com or visit the site office from Monday to Saturday between 10 am and 6 pm. Every request gets a ticket number by email. The customer care desk replies within 2 working days.

## Escalation to the CRM head
If a request is not resolved within 5 working days, the buyer can escalate it by quoting the ticket number to crm.head@example.com. The CRM head replies within 3 working days.

## Grievance officer
If the CRM head's reply does not resolve the issue, the buyer can write to the grievance officer at grievance@example.com. The grievance officer gives a written decision within 10 working days.

## Complaint to the regulator
A buyer can also file a complaint with the state Real Estate Regulatory Authority or its adjudicating officer at any time. Using the developer's escalation steps first is optional, not required.

## Emergencies after possession
For lift breakdowns, water leaks, power failure or fire alarms after possession, residents call the 24-hour facility office at the intercom extension 9.
