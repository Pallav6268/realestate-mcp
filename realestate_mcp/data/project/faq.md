# Riverside Heights — Buyer FAQ

## Project overview
Riverside Heights is a residential project by Sunrise Habitat Developers Pvt. Ltd. in New Town, Kolkata. It has three towers (A, B and C), each with ground plus 14 floors, and 2 BHK and 3 BHK apartments with carpet areas from 950 to 1,250 sq ft. This project, the developer and every buyer named in this demo are fictional.

## RERA registration
The project is registered with the state Real Estate Regulatory Authority under the demo registration number RH-DEMO-0001 (fictional). The registration number is printed on every advertisement, brochure and demand letter.

## Booking process
To book an apartment, the buyer pays the booking amount of 10% of the total consideration and signs the Agreement for Sale. The developer does not accept more than 10% of the cost of the apartment before the Agreement for Sale is signed and registered.

## Documents needed for booking
Buyers need a PAN card, an address proof, two passport-size photographs and a cheque or bank transfer for the booking amount. Joint buyers each provide their own PAN card and address proof.

## Home loans
Buyers may take a home loan from any bank or housing finance company. The developer issues the documents a lender usually asks for, such as the allotment letter, a no-objection certificate and the payment schedule, within 7 working days of a written request.

## Car parking
Every apartment comes with one covered car parking space at no additional cost. An additional covered space, where available, is allotted on a first-come, first-served basis at ₹ 3,50,000.

## Site visits
Buyers can visit the site from Monday to Saturday between 10 am and 5 pm after booking a slot with the customer care desk. Safety helmets are provided and children under 12 are not allowed on active construction floors.

## Construction updates
The developer sends a construction progress update with photographs to every buyer by email in the first week of every quarter.
