# Riverside Heights — Payment Plan

## Construction-linked payment schedule
The total consideration is paid in four instalments: 10% on booking, 40% on completion of the structure, 45% on completion of finishing works and 5% on possession.

## Demand letters
For each instalment after booking, the developer sends a demand letter by email and post with an engineer's certificate confirming the construction milestone. Payment is due within 15 days of the date of the demand letter.

## Interest on late payment
If an instalment is paid after its due date, the buyer pays interest at 9% per annum on the delayed amount for the period of delay. This is the same rate the developer pays the buyer for delay in possession.

## Taxes and government charges
Stamp duty, registration fees and GST are not part of the total consideration and are payable by the buyer as applicable under law at the time of payment.

## Payment modes
Payments are accepted by cheque, demand draft or bank transfer to the project's designated bank account named in the demand letter. The developer never asks for payment in cash or to a personal account.

## Receipts
A receipt is emailed within 3 working days of each payment being credited. A consolidated statement of account can be requested from the customer care desk at any time.
