# Standard Agreement for Sale — Riverside Heights (Template)

## 1. Parties
This Agreement for Sale is made between Sunrise Habitat Developers Pvt. Ltd. (the Promoter) and {{allottee_name}} (the Allottee).

## 2. Property
Apartment No. {{unit_no}} on floor {{floor}} of Tower {{tower}}, Riverside Heights, New Town, Kolkata, with a carpet area of {{carpet_area}} sq ft.

## 3. Consideration
The total consideration for the Apartment is {{total_price}}, of which a booking amount of 10% of the total consideration is payable on signing this Agreement.

## 4. Payment Schedule
The Allottee shall pay 10% on booking, 40% on completion of the structure, 45% on completion of finishing works and 5% on possession.

## 5. Possession
The Promoter shall hand over possession of the Apartment on or before 31 March 2028, with a grace period of 6 months only for reasons beyond its control.

## 6. Delay Compensation
If possession is delayed beyond the grace period, the Promoter shall pay the Allottee interest at 9% per annum on the amount paid, for every month of delay.

## 7. Maintenance
Maintenance charges of ₹ 3.50 per sq ft per month are payable from the date of possession, with 12 months payable in advance.

## 8. Cancellation by Allottee
If the Allottee cancels this Agreement, the Promoter may forfeit 10% of the total consideration and shall refund the balance within 45 days.

## 9. Car Parking
One covered car parking space is allotted with the Apartment at no additional cost.

## 10. Dispute Resolution
Disputes shall first be settled amicably within 30 days, failing which they shall be referred to arbitration seated in Kolkata.

## 11. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts at Kolkata shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
